The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. This is a simple test file to verify that the parallel reader can correctly load and process text files.

The parallel reader supports multiple file formats including PDF, EPUB, FB2, and plain text files. When you load a text file, the application will extract the text, split it into sentences, and translate each sentence from English to Russian.

You can click on any word to pause the reading and see its translation highlighted in green. Double-click on a word to start reading from that position. The application automatically saves your reading position, so you can continue where you left off even after closing the app.
